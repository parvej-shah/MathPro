## 1. Target Audience & ICP Breakdown

### Target Persona 1: The Student (JSC, SSC '26/'27, HSC '26–'28)
- **Bangla Medium**: Needs to overcome math fear, avoid blind memorization, master creative questions (CQ), and gain speed in MCQs.
- **English Version**: Underserved in Bangladesh; needs accurate English math terminology, step-by-step proofs, and a mentor who teaches fluently without language barriers.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Unclear foundations from earlier grades leading to anxiety.
  - Large offline coaching classes where questions are discouraged.
  - Lack of timely doubt support when doing homework.

### Target Persona 2: The Parent (The Economic Buyer)
- **Pain Points**:
  - Inefficient commuting and traffic for offline batches.
  - Unverified private tutors lacking systematic routine.
- **Desires**:
  - Proven instructor credibility (Dhaka University alumni, 8+ years Udvas track record).
  - Consistent exam routine (daily quizzes, board-standard model tests).
  - High academic ROI (Golden A+ in Board Exams).
